# SEMMA in Practice: Customer Segmentation with K-Means

## Abstract

This project demonstrates the SAS-inspired SEMMA process for mall customer segmentation.

## Workflow

The sample is inspected, distributions and relationships are explored, income and spending score are standardized, K-Means candidates are compared, and the resulting clusters are profiled.

## Results

The executed run selects five clusters by silhouette score among candidates from two through eight. Cluster profiles are useful hypotheses for differentiated marketing, but they must be tested against future campaign outcomes.

## Reproducibility

The notebook saves the model-selection table, segment assignments, and visualization under `SEMMA/artifacts/`.
