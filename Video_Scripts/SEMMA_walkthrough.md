# SEMMA walkthrough script (3–5 minutes)

1. Introduce the mall-customer segmentation question.
2. Show Sample and Explore: schema, distributions, and pair plot.
3. Explain Modify: feature selection and standardization.
4. Show the inertia/silhouette table for candidate k values.
5. Explain why k=5 was selected and show the segment plot.
6. Close with cautious marketing hypotheses, stability checks, and limitations.
